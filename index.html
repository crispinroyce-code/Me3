About us

Our mission is to provide daily fresh baked pastries, breakfast and lunch to our community, for families to enjoy time together with good food and feel comfortable in a relaxed environment.

We serve both Ghanaian and American meals. We bake our own burger buns, squeeze our own juices with no added colouring, and make every cake to order.
